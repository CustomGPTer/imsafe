Release notes

What changed in each version of ImSafe. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.1 21 November 2026Latest

  • Installs without Google Play Protect blocking it. The two parts it objected to have been taken out: the volume-button panic trigger (shake and the side button still work) and the option to disguise the app's icon.
  • Text that was hard to read on the dark theme, on Home and the PIN keypad, now shows clearly.
  • After the right PIN at “Are you OK?”, the countdown stops straight away while you choose what to do next.
  • Updating keeps your contacts, places and settings. If the ImSafe icon goes from your home screen, add it again from the app drawer.

Version 1.0 21 November 2026

  • Photo and Taxi: one press sends your location to your contacts as you take the picture, with the number plate read on the phone if you like.
  • Photo links through your own Google Drive, uploaded once and shared only with your contacts.
  • Up to five contacts, each sent an invite you see first. A YES reply marks them as accepted.
  • Texts from your own number, with Sent and Delivered for each contact, and retries for up to a day without signal.
  • Walk me home: saved places, an OpenStreetMap map with address and postcode search, or a plain timer. Ends by itself when you arrive.
  • “Are you OK?” check with an alarm over the lock screen, then texts and calls on speaker to each contact in turn, with location updates until you cancel.
  • Danger PIN that looks like a normal cancel but sends a hidden alert.
  • Hold mode, hold-for-help, a low-battery text and a text if the phone is switched off during a walk.
  • Optional checks for stopping or leaving your route.
  • Panic triggers: shake, the side button or the volume button, each with an optional countdown.
  • Siren with flashing torch and screen, and a fake incoming call from the name you choose.
  • Lone working in Settings › Advanced, with check-ins and optional fall detection.
  • Test mode, disguise as Calculator, Notes or Weather, three home-screen widgets and a quick settings tile.
  • Editable message wording, history, guides to every feature, and a dark or light look.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.1 is called ImSafe.apk. It is 26.7 MB (26,651,467 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

25f2f4da7a3c6b3a8d5c67cd04d23c8981531c5cae8315437981a60f04aad8dd

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile ImSafe.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps your contacts and settings.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.

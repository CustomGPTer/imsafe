ImSafe 1.0 is the first public release of ImSafe, a free personal safety app for Android 10 or later.

- Photo and Taxi: one press sends your location to your chosen contacts as you take the picture, with optional number plate reading on the phone.
- Photo links through your own Google Drive.
- Walk me home: saved places, a map with address and postcode search, or a plain timer. Ends by itself when you arrive.
- "Are you OK?" check over the lock screen, then texts and calls on speaker to each contact in turn, with location updates until you cancel.
- Danger PIN, hold mode, hold for help, and texts when the battery is low or the phone is switched off during a walk.
- Panic triggers (shake, side button, volume button), siren, and a fake incoming call.
- Lone working with check-ins and optional fall detection, in Settings > Advanced.
- Test mode, disguise, three home-screen widgets and a quick settings tile.

ImSafe is not an emergency service. If you're in danger, call 999.

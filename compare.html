How ImSafe compares

Other safety apps record video or offer paid monitoring centres. ImSafe does the essentials well, for free: it texts and rings the people who know you, from your own number.

What ImSafe does not do

ImSafe has no monitoring centre and doesn't contact the police for you: it tells the people you choose. It doesn't record audio or video, and it doesn't stream your location live to a map; your contacts get map links by text instead. It is an Android app only, and it isn't on Google Play.

Why ImSafe works the way it does

Everything goes by ordinary text and phone call from your own number, so your contacts don't need an app or an account, and there's no server in the middle that could go down, be sold or be hacked. The cost is that ImSafe needs phone signal, like any call.

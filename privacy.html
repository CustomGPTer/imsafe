Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

ImSafe is a free Android app made by Jonathan Jackson. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there's no ImSafe account, no server of ours and no tracking. Your information stays on your phone, in your own text messages and in your own Google Drive.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name, PIN (stored only as a secure scrambled code), settings and up to five contacts' names and numbers.
  • A history of what ImSafe has sent and whether it was delivered.
  • Photos you take in the app, unless you choose to have them deleted once they're in your Drive.

None of this is sent to us. Android's own cloud backup is switched off for ImSafe, so it isn't copied there either. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your location

ImSafe looks for your location only when you send something. It's put into the text message to your chosen contacts as a Google Maps link. ImSafe doesn't track you in the background and doesn't send your location anywhere else.

Text messages

Messages are ordinary texts sent from your own phone number to the contacts you choose. If you allow ImSafe to read replies, it looks only for a YES or NO from a contact you've invited, to mark them as accepted. Every other text is ignored and nothing is stored.

Camera and number plates

Photos are taken only when you press the button. If you turn on plate reading, the plate is read on your phone using Google's ML Kit, which works offline. The photo isn't sent anywhere for that.

Google Drive and your Google data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, ImSafe asks for the narrowest Drive permission Google offers (drive.file). That lets ImSafe see and manage only the files it creates itself. It can't see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

  • What ImSafe does: creates a folder called "ImSafe photos" in your Drive, uploads each photo you take in the app, and turns on "anyone with the link can view" for that photo so your contacts can open it. It also reads your Google account's email address to show which account is connected.
  • Who gets the link: only the contacts you've chosen, by text message.
  • What we receive: nothing. The upload goes straight from your phone to your own Drive. No Google data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Removing it: delete photos or the folder in Google Drive at any time. Tap Disconnect in ImSafe's settings to withdraw access, or remove ImSafe at myaccount.google.com/connections.

ImSafe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the photo-link feature you can see in the app. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to train AI models.

Children

ImSafe isn't aimed at children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions can be raised on the ImSafe GitHub page.

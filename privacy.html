Privacy policy

What ImSafe does with your information, in plain English.

Last updated 21 November 2026

ImSafe is a free Android app made by Jonathan Jackson. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there's no ImSafe account, no server of ours and no tracking. Your information stays on your phone, in your own text messages and calls, and in your own Google Drive.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name, PIN and danger PIN (each stored only as a secure scrambled code), settings, saved places and up to five contacts' names and numbers.
  • A history of what ImSafe has sent and whether it was delivered, and of your last 50 walks, shifts and alerts.
  • Photos you take in the app, unless you choose to have them deleted once they're in your Drive.

None of this is sent to us. Android's own cloud backup is switched off for ImSafe, so it isn't copied there either. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your location

ImSafe looks for your location when you send something, and while a walk, a lone-working shift or an alert is running. When you send something, your location goes into the text to your chosen contacts as a Google Maps link.

During a walk or shift, ImSafe follows your location on the phone so it can tell when you've arrived and, only if you've switched these checks on, whether you've stopped moving or left your route. A notification shows the whole time. Your location is sent to your contacts only if you've asked ImSafe to tell them you've set off, if your battery runs low or the phone is switched off during a walk, or if an alert starts. When the walk or shift ends, ImSafe stops following your location. It never sends your location to us or anyone else.

Searching for a place

When you search for a destination on the map, the words you type are sent to postcodes.io (for UK postcodes) or to OpenStreetMap's search service, which send back matching places. When you drop a pin, its position is sent to OpenStreetMap's search service to look up the street name. The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap. None of these services gets your name, your contacts or anything else from ImSafe.

Phone calls and texts

Messages are ordinary texts sent from your own phone number to the contacts you choose. If an alert starts and you've left calls switched on, ImSafe rings your contacts one at a time from your own phone, on speaker. If you allow “Phone status”, ImSafe uses it only to tell when each call has finished so it can ring the next contact. It doesn't read your call history.

If you allow ImSafe to read replies, it looks only for a YES or NO from a contact you've invited, to mark them as accepted. Every other text is ignored and nothing is stored.

Motion, buttons and the fake call

If you switch on shake as a panic trigger, or fall detection for lone working, ImSafe reads the phone's motion sensor on the phone itself. Nothing about your movement is recorded or sent anywhere. If you switch on the volume-button trigger, Android asks you to turn on ImSafe's accessibility service. It is used only to count presses of the volume-down button; it doesn't read anything on your screen and doesn't change what your buttons do. The fake call happens only on your phone: nobody is called and no one is told.

Camera and number plates

Photos are taken only when you press the button. If you turn on plate reading, the plate is read on your phone using Google's ML Kit, which works offline. The photo isn't sent anywhere for that.

Google Drive and your Google data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, ImSafe asks for the narrowest Drive permission Google offers (drive.file). That lets ImSafe see and manage only the files it creates itself. It can't see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

  • What ImSafe does: creates a folder called “ImSafe photos” in your Drive, uploads each photo you take in the app, and turns on “anyone with the link can view” for that photo so your contacts can open it. It also reads your Google account's email address to show which account is connected.
  • Who gets the link: only the contacts you've chosen, by text message.
  • What we receive: nothing. The upload goes straight from your phone to your own Drive. No Google data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Removing it: delete photos or the folder in Google Drive at any time. Tap Disconnect in ImSafe's settings to withdraw access, or remove ImSafe at myaccount.google.com/connections.

ImSafe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the photo-link feature you can see in the app. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to train AI models.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

ImSafe isn't aimed at children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions can be raised on the ImSafe GitHub page.

Last updated 21 November 2026

Not an emergency service

ImSafe sends messages and makes calls to people you choose. It doesn't contact the emergency services, and nobody monitors your alerts. If you or anyone else is in danger, call 999 (or your local emergency number).

No guarantee

ImSafe depends on your phone, its battery, its settings and permissions, your mobile network, location services and, for photo links, Google Drive. Messages and calls can be delayed or fail, locations can be inaccurate, panic triggers and fall detection can miss or misfire, and phones can stop apps in the background. ImSafe is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Don't rely on it as your only means of staying safe, and test it in Test mode before you need it.

Your responsibilities

  • Only add contacts who have agreed to receive messages and calls from you.
  • Use the camera, the siren and the fake call lawfully and with respect for other people.
  • Texts and calls are charged by your mobile network at your normal rate.
